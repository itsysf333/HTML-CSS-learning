Classic Lasagna

Prep: 30m | Serves: 6

Classic lasagna is a rich and comforting Italian dish made by layering flat pasta sheets with a savory meat sauce (ragù), creamy béchamel or ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella. Baked until bubbly and golden brown, it is a timeless comfort food favorite.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Tomato sauce

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles.
  2. Brown the ground beef and add sauce.
  3. Layer meat, noodles, and cheese.
  4. Bake until golden brown.

Tips

Cover with foil for the first 20 minutes to prevent the cheese from burning.
